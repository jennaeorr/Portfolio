Capstone Project

Arizona State University | 2023

For my year-long capstone at Arizona State University, I worked with Medecipher Solutions, a company dedicated to improving clinical operations in hospitals through advanced decision-support tools. Medecipher identified a critical challenge across healthcare systems: nurse schedulers lack an efficient, intuitive platform for creating balanced staffing plans, resulting in understaffing, burnout, and inconsistent patient care. Our goal was to develop a scheduling solution that is simple, data-driven, and capable of optimizing staffing decisions at both the daily and hourly levels.

I contributed to the development of several key components of the platform. This included creating dashboards that visualize nurse staffing patterns, including net staffing levels, shortfall/surplus charts, and hourly breakdowns pulled directly from the database. For example, the Net Staffing and Surplus/Shortfall visualizations (Figures 1.0 and 1.1) provide schedulers with clear insight into staffing imbalances by distinguishing active nurses from those on breaks or lunch and highlighting when staffing exceeds or falls short of demand.

I also designed and implemented the Rapid ROI table (Figure 1.2), which tracks FTE types, unproductive rates, FMLA weeks, and patient acuity levels using the Emergency Severity Index (ESI). Each field is integrated with a robust data-validation system to ensure accurate inputs and reliable outputs.

Additional project responsibilities included developing the nurse characteristic table with pop-ups and bulk-upload functionality, implementing dashboard metrics for model runs and forecasting, and building several D3.js-powered charts used to display staffing forecasts, performance statistics, and summary metrics. I also contributed to UI improvements throughout the application, refactoring pages for clarity, organization, and usability.

Throughout this project, I strengthened my skills in Angular, D3.js, HTML/CSS, JSON, VSCode, Docker, Node.js, Taiga, and Amazon Web Services, while gaining experience in developing data-visualization-driven applications for real-world operational challenges.
