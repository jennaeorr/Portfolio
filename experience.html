Capstone Project

Arizona State University | 2023

For my year-long capstone at Arizona State University, I worked with Medecipher Solutions, a company dedicated to improving clinical operations in hospitals through advanced decision-support tools. Medecipher identified a critical challenge: nurse schedulers lacked an efficient, intuitive platform for creating balanced staffing plans, resulting in understaffing, burnout, and inconsistent patient care. Our goal was to develop a simple, data-driven solution capable of optimizing staffing decisions at both the daily and hourly levels.

I contributed to key components, including dashboards visualizing nurse staffing patterns, net staffing levels, and surplus/shortfall charts pulled directly from the database. For example, Net Staffing and Surplus/Shortfall visualizations (Figures 1.0 and 1.1) provide schedulers insight into staffing imbalances by highlighting active nurses versus those on breaks and showing staffing deviations.

I also designed and implemented the Rapid ROI table (Figure 1.2), which tracks FTE types, unproductive rates, FMLA weeks, and patient acuity levels using the Emergency Severity Index (ESI). All inputs integrate a robust data-validation system to ensure accurate analysis.

Additional responsibilities included developing the nurse characteristic table with pop-ups and bulk-upload functionality, implementing dashboard metrics for model runs and forecasting, and building D3.js-powered charts for staffing forecasts, performance statistics, and summary metrics. UI improvements, page refactoring, and styling enhancements were also part of my contributions.

Throughout this project, I strengthened my skills in Angular, D3.js, HTML/CSS, JSON, VSCode, Docker, Node.js, Taiga, and AWS, while gaining experience in creating data-visualization-driven applications for real-world operational challenges.
